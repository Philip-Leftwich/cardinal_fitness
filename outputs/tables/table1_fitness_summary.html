Each cell compares the genotype with the wildtype (WT) group of the same line and experiment: ↓/↑, significantly lower/higher than WT; ≈, no significant difference (p ≥ 0.05). The number is the effect ratio relative to WT: odds ratio (larva-to-adult survival, hatching, Smurf), rate ratio (eggs per female) or time ratio (survival after a blood meal). * p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001 (Dunnett-adjusted within line for eclosion, fecundity and hatching; Holm-adjusted within line for survival). † Ratio not estimable because one group had no events (no deaths or no Smurf+ females); significance from a log-rank or Fisher’s exact test. ‡ Not estimable: cdg225 heterozygous females died after blood feeding or did not feed. § Only 2 females. — Not assessed. WT groups: cdg384 eclosion and fecundity, non-transgenic siblings; cd384R eclosion and fecundity, SDA-500; cdg225 fecundity, siblings pooled with SDA-500; survival after blood feeding, non-transgenic siblings of each line (cd225R HET and trans-heterozygotes: cdg225 WT siblings). Transgenic mother = transgenic female × SDA-500 male; transgenic father = SDA-500 female × transgenic male. Observed counts, 95% CIs and exact p-values are given in Supplementary Table S_fitness.
